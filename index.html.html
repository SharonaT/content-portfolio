About me

Content that is planned, written, checked and measured.

I lead content and marketing for B2B businesses at Maverick Communications International. I run a team of 10 and manage about €480k in annual marketing budgets, plus €350k+ in media, for clients in financial services, software, MedTech and manufacturing.

My most recent work is Perigord CCM, a customer communications provider for regulated financial services, insurance and healthcare organisations. I wrote and edited its website, set its brand voice and run its monthly content calendar. I am now building its marketing strategy for Ireland and the UK.

I plan content from the first brief through to measurement: websites, product use cases, white papers, case studies, newsletters, video, PR and webinars. Technical content I produce goes through expert and compliance review before it is published. Earlier in my career I led online strategy and content teams in media, and at Hewlett Packard Enterprise I worked with country marketing teams across EMEA and LATAM. I also speak at industry events and have been a podcast guest.
